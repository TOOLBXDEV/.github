Overview

Every campaign lives on the GTM Campaign object in HubSpot. Contacts, companies and deals are associated with campaigns, and each association carries labels describing what happened.

  • Contacts are labelled from recorded activity (replies, connected calls, meetings, forms, attendance and conversation notes) inside each campaign's attribution window.
  • A contact becomes an MQL the first time a campaign qualifies them. That date is mos_mql_date.
  • An account becomes an MQA when its first contact becomes an MQL (mos_mqa_date).
  • An SQL is a deal created in the Sales Pipeline. A HIRO is an SQL that reached Solution Review.
  • Each deal is credited to one campaign through Primary deal source.
  • The reconciler sets the contact labels and the MQL and MQA dates. Anything that would add a contact to a campaign or make them an MQL waits for approval in the Marketing OS review queue.
  • MQL and MQA dates before 1 September 2026 are locked.

How is_icp is determined

is_icp is set by a HubSpot workflow that evaluates company properties against ICP criteria. The logic includes a product-category vertical branch that is still work in progress. Until the vertical logic ships, some companies may carry a stale ICP status.

ICP status revocation

If is_icp is cleared on a company, existing MQL and MQA dates on its contacts and the company itself are not removed: those dates reflect that the contact qualified when the company was ICP. Reporting filters on current ICP status, so revoked companies drop out of reported counts automatically. The reconciler will not create new MQLs for contacts at non-ICP companies.

The rule

A contact qualifies on a campaign when, inside its window, at least one of these holds:

  • Status is Attendee or Engaged, and the evidence is more than a click
  • They have a Depth label above Light touch
  • The campaign produced a meeting or a deal and there's recorded activity behind it

Reported MQLs are then limited to contacts at ICP companies.

The contact's MQL fields

FieldValue
mos_mql_dateThe earliest qualifying date across all campaigns. Set once; it can move earlier if earlier evidence arrives, never later.
mos_mql_last_dateThe latest qualifying date
mos_mql_basisThe strongest basis across campaigns: engagement (a recorded activity), attendance (attended, nothing else recorded) or deal-inferred (below). Never downgrades.

Deal-inferred MQLs

When a campaign sources a deal and no contact on the deal qualified within 7 days of it opening, one contact on the deal (ICP first) is made an MQL, dated to the deal's create date, with basis deal-inferred. The system never overwrites an existing date. See open item 2.

Re-engagement

An MQL who goes 210 days or more without a qualifying touch and then comes back is re-engaged, not a new MQL. mos_mql_reengaged_date holds the latest return and mos_mql_reengaged_count the number of returns. Re-engagements are reported as a separate line, never added to MQLs.

Why 210 days. Two thresholds from our own data:

  • The longest silence seen inside a live deal is 199 days.
  • 95% of MQLs that become deals do so within 209 days.

210 is the first threshold that never fires on someone still in a buying cycle. A 180-day threshold misfires once in a live deal, and 90 days misfires 25 times.

Qualification

MQA

mos_mqa_date is the earliest mos_mql_date among the contacts whose primary company is the account. It can move earlier if an earlier-dated contact appears, never later. Only ICP accounts are reported. See open item 3 for a proposed tighter definition.

MQA gap risk

Because the reconciler runs weekly (daily for recent activity, once built), there can be a lag between a contact becoming an MQL and the company's mos_mqa_date being set. During this window, reports that count MQAs may undercount by the unprocessed MQLs. The daily run (building) will narrow the lag to <24 hours for recent activity. Until then, expect up to a 7-day delay between MQL and MQA for contacts processed in the weekly full run.

Qualification

SQL and HIRO

SQL

A deal created in the Sales Pipeline at an ICP company, dated by its create date. Deals enter at Meeting Booked. dealtype separates new business from expansion; renewals sit in their own pipeline and don't count.

HIRO

An SQL that reached Solution Review or later, dated by when it entered Solution Review. Pipeline value is the sum of annual_recurring_revenue_w_est on HIRO deals.

Stage order: Meeting Booked → Solution Review → Proposal Review → Negotiation → Contract Sent → Closed Won. On contact–campaign links the SQL outcome is IO: Deal created. HubSpot's Sales Qualified Lead lifecycle stage is sales-owned and isn't used to count SQLs.

How credit is decided

  1. Primary contact. A deal's campaigns come through its primary contact, the person who started the conversation. Other contacts on the deal don't add campaigns.
  2. Evidence. A touch earns credit only if its label records an actual interaction (Light touch or stronger). Targeted, No show and Pending earn none.
  3. Window. The deal must be created within the campaign's window.
  4. Proximity. A touch more than 90 days before the deal can be Influenced by, never Primary deal source. If no touch is close enough, the deal is influence-only.
  5. Sales-sourced. If no campaign qualifies and a rep connected first (an answered call, a reply or a meeting), the deal goes to that quarter's Sales Prospecting (YYYY-QN) campaign.

Multi-touch weighting (W-shape): one touch 100%; two 50/50; three 30/40/30; four or more 30% first, 30% key middle, 30% last, and 10% across the rest.

Reporting lock: before 1 September 2026

MQL and MQA dates before 1 September 2026 are final: confirmed and shared with investors. The reconciler never moves or clears them, or changes their basis. An MQL or MQA found now whose true date falls before the lock is dated 1 September 2026. Labels aren't locked. Every full run prints reported MQLs by month (current → after the run) and the number of locked months affected, which must be 0.

Operations

Running the reconciler

scripts/reconcile-campaign-labels.ts works out the complete label set for every contact–campaign and company–campaign link and replaces what's there. It reads campaigns, associations, contact lifecycle history, deals, and email, call, meeting and note activity. It never edits deals or deal attribution labels.

# dry run (default): full report, writes nothing
npx tsx scripts/reconcile-campaign-labels.ts

# send held changes to the review queue
npx tsx scripts/reconcile-campaign-labels.ts --propose=queue.json
npx tsx scripts/import-attribution-proposals.ts queue.json --apply

# write all changes that don't need review
CONFIRM_APPLY=yes npx tsx scripts/reconcile-campaign-labels.ts --apply
OptionEffect
--applyWrites. Requires CONFIRM_APPLY=yes.
--propose=fileWrites held changes to a file for the importer instead
--include-reviewAlso writes held changes (for deliberate backfills only)
--campaign=id · --only=dimensionLimits the run. MQL dates aren't written on a limited run.
--json=fileExports every proposed change for review
--mql-out=fileExports every contact's MQL date after the run

A full dry run takes about 15–20 minutes, and a full apply about 40. Before applying, check the dry run's month table: locked months affected must be 0.

Operations

Open items

Scope evidence to the campaign

Only conversation notes are tied to a specific campaign today. Any other qualifying activity inside a window counts for every campaign the contact is on. A sales call four weeks after a show can qualify the contact on the show, even if nobody spoke to them there.

Proposed:

  • Event campaigns count only attendance, conversation notes, and meetings or forms during the event.
  • Outreach campaigns count replies to their own sends.
  • Sales calls and meetings not tied to a campaign count for that quarter's Sales Prospecting campaign.

Stop creating deal-inferred MQLs

These make an MQL out of a deal, without any marketing evidence. That makes the funnel add up, but it hides the case it should show: an SQL no MQL came before.

Proposed: from September, stop creating them, and report "SQLs without an MQL" as its own line. Existing ones before the lock stay.

Make MQA a multi-contact threshold

Marketing-led accounts, excluding MQAs created by the deal itself, convert to a deal within a year in line with how many contacts qualified first:

MQL contacts on the accountAccountsBecame a deal
125718%
25330%
3+2040%

Proposed: MQA = an ICP account with 2+ MQL contacts within 180 days, dated by the second. The current single-contact count becomes "engaged accounts".

Record the SQL date on the contact

Approved. mos_sql_date on the deal's primary contact, set to the deal's create date. This lets contact-level MQL → SQL timing be measured without changing how SQLs are counted.

Mark the primary contact on deals

Until this exists, the attribution scan reads every contact on a deal.

Proposed: a Primary contact label on the deal–contact association. AEs set this when they create the deal; Sales Ops supports with training and auditing. Attribution then reads it directly.

One writer for company breadth labels

The reconciler and a HubSpot workflow both write Single / Multi-contact. The workflow adds labels without removing them.

Status: left as is for now.

Record the latest MQA date on the company

Proposed: mos_mqa_last_date on the company, set to the latest mos_mql_date among its primary contacts. Mirrors mos_mql_last_date at the account level for re-engagement and recency analysis.

Flag for Connor Connected calls exclude "Gatekeeper" outcomes

Calls whose outcome is "Gatekeeper" are not treated as qualifying evidence, even if they meet the 2-minute threshold. Confirm this exclusion is correctly applied in call-activity scoring.

Flag for Connor Meetings exclude no-shows

Meetings where the prospect did not attend are excluded from qualifying evidence. Confirm no-show detection logic and how it interacts with the meeting booking date vs. meeting date evidence rule.

Flag for Connor New Logo and Pipeline mutual exclusivity

The Intent labels New Logo and Pipeline should be mutually exclusive. A contact in active pipeline at a campaign's start should never also be labelled New Logo. Confirm the reconciler enforces this.

Flag for Connor Who sets IO: Disqualified

Disqualified is set by hand today. Determine who should own setting this label (Marketing, Sales Ops or AEs) and whether any guardrails or approval flow are needed.
